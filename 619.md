# Vision

619 is not a reminder application.

619 is a premium Islamic Productivity Platform designed to help Muslims become more disciplined in both Deen and Dunya.

The application combines:

- Habit Tracking
- Goal Management
- Islamic Daily Companion
- Motivation
- Streaks
- Smart Reminders
- Daily Progress

The user should feel:

"I am becoming a better Muslim and a more disciplined person."

NOT

"This is another reminder app."
